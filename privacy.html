What this project is

Elmirix is a frontend-only university project demonstration. Sample business names and figures are fictional. This page explains the demo's intended behavior and is not legal advice or a formal privacy policy.

Information you enter

The contact form validates fields in your browser but does not send or store its contents. Demo inquiries are saved in this browser's local storage so the investor and business-owner dashboards can demonstrate status tracking; they are not sent to a business or server. Avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, or account information.

Local browser storage

The demo may store saved business IDs, recently viewed sample IDs, and profile fields in this browser's local storage. Profile fields can include the name and email entered on registration, business profile edits and locally previewed profile images, investor preferences, financial-record entries, funding-request drafts, and demo inquiry messages with contact details. These are demo records, not accounts, and are not sent to a server. Passwords and phone numbers are never stored. Avoid real or confidential details; clear this site's browser storage to remove demo records.

The selected demo role and session name/email may remain in this browser tab's session storage until you exit the demo or close the tab.

External services

The page may load fonts and interface libraries from external content-delivery services. Their availability and handling are outside this project's control. Opening the pages from a simple local static server is recommended for consistent behavior.

No financial or identity service

This project does not provide secure authentication, investment transactions, payments, identity verification, or independently verified financial records. Nothing here is an investment, legal, or financial recommendation.

Questions

For project-related questions, use the contact page. Its form is simulated and does not send your message.
