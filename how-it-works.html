A CLEAR PATH FORWARD

How Elmirix works.

A simple discovery experience helps business owners share context and gives investors a place to begin exploring.

HomeHow It Works

FOR BUSINESS OWNERS

Share your idea with context.

Build a picture of your business, explain what you are working toward, and make space for a useful conversation.

01

Create a business profile

Introduce your business, its location, industry, and stage.

02

Describe your work

Explain your products, market, mission, and business goals.

03

Present available information

Share financial summaries with clear reporting periods and context.

04

Set a funding goal

Outline an intended target and explain how funds could support growth.

05

Manage inquiries

Review questions and keep conversations organized in the demo experience.

These actions are simulated and saved only in this browser; messages are not delivered and no funding is transacted.

FOR INVESTORS

Explore opportunities with care.

Browse profiles, review the information available, and use direct conversations to learn more.

01

Browse businesses

Explore sample businesses from a range of industries.

02

Filter to your interests

Narrow by sector, location, stage, and available figures.

03

Compare information

Review each profile and note when information is missing.

04

Save promising ideas

Keep a local demo bookmark list for profiles you want to revisit.

05

Start a conversation

Preview how a question to a business owner could be initiated.
